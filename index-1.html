03 — Process

Two weeks,
four steps.

No jargon, no monthly agency retainer, no 40-page decks. You talk to me, the person writing the code.

  1. 01

    Teardown

    A 20-minute call, then a free screen-recorded teardown of your current site: what's costing you calls, and what I'd do about it. No obligation.

  2. 02

    Blueprint

    We agree the pages, the copy direction and the one action you want visitors to take. You approve a single-clickable design before a line of code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-coded pages, your photos and copy, real browser testing on phones, tablets and desktops. You watch it come together on a private link.

  4. 04

    Launch & care

    Domain, hosting, Google Business profile and analytics set up. Then your monthly plan covers the edits, backups, security updates and uptime checks.
